## Brand & Style

The design system embodies the audacious opulence, sensual subversion, and Paris-at-midnight decadence of an ultra-luxury couture gala. Built for high-net-worth VIPs, couture clientele, and international fashion press, the interface must evoke an immediate sensation of exclusivity, dramatic ceremony, and celebratory indulgence.

The visual style unites **High-Contrast Couture Lacquer** with **Gilded Glassmorphism**:
- **Glossy Midnight Depths:** Deep obsidian surfaces mimic hand-poured grand piano lacquer and vinyl lip stains.
- **Imperial Shimmer:** Micro-particle gold dust effects, metallic leaf hairline trims, and rich velvet burgundy undertones.
- **Architectural Poise:** Dramatic verticality, stark typographic scale contrasts, and generous breathing room evoking luxury editorial lookbooks and engraved gala invitations.

## Colors

The palette operates strictly in a dramatic dark mode, prioritizing chromatic tension between pitch black, blood burgundy, and lustrous champagne gold.

### Roles & Accents
- **Primary (`#D4AF37` Imperial Champagne Gold):** Key interactive focal points, gala branding, fine borders, and celebratory micro-accents. Supported by `#FFDF73` (highlight shimmer) and `#AA7C11` (burnished gold hover states).
- **Secondary (`#6B0515` Deep Velvet Burgundy):** Dramatic surface washes, high-value container fills, and radiant radial backdrop glows. Anchored by `#4A000B` for low-tier shadows and `#990000` for illuminated vignettes.
- **Tertiary (`#B91C1C` Blood Cherry Accent):** Used sparingly for urgent live alerts, RSVP confirmations, active indicators, and high-impact visual spikes.
- **Neutral (`#0A0708` Deep Midnight Noir):** The primary canvas ground, supplemented by `#14090C` for raised lacquer cards and `#FBFBFB` (Holiday Pearl White) strictly for pristine editorial text and contrast legibility.

## Typography

The typographical hierarchy mirrors Parisian haute couture publishing: high-contrast editorial serifs (`Playfair Display`) paired with clean, tracked geometric sans-serifs (`Montserrat`).

- **Serif Application:** Display and headline levels rely on `Playfair Display` with deliberate italic moments for romantic couture emphasis (e.g., *Le Smoking*, *L'Éclat*).
- **Tracked Capitals:** All metadata, section eyebrows, CTAs, table reservations, and badge indicators utilize `label-caps-lg` or `label-caps-sm` with rigorous uppercase letter-spacing (0.18em to 0.22em).
- **Body Styling:** Body text remains understated, set in light-to-regular weights with airy line heights to ensure legible, museum-grade layout balance against black lacquered backdrops.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is structured upon a refined 12-column fluid grid system designed for expansive white (noir) space and theatrical pacing.

### Layout Principles
- **Grid Structure:** 12 columns across desktop with 1.5rem gutters; collapsible to 6 columns on tablet (2rem margins) and 4 columns on mobile (1rem gutters, 1.25rem margins).
- **Breathing Room:** Key promotional hero pieces and gala showcases must use expansive section gaps (`space-xl` scaled dynamically up to 6rem on wide displays) to preserve the aura of bespoke exclusivity.
- **Asymmetry:** Visual weight should alternate intentionally—dramatic full-bleed photography offset by narrow, highly structured typographic panels.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy uses a couture blend of **translucent lacquer** and **warm specular lighting** instead of standard shadow drops.

### Depth Mechanics
- **Base Canvas:** Solid `#0A0708` infused with ambient radial gradients of `#6B0515` (15% to 30% opacity) positioned behind focal points.
- **Lacquered Glass:** Elevated overlays and invitation cards use `rgba(20, 9, 12, 0.72)` paired with `backdrop-filter: blur(24px) saturate(140%)`.
- **Couture Hairline Framing:** Containers elevate through a 1px solid or gradient border: `linear-gradient(135deg, rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.6) 0%, rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.1) 50%, rgba(185, 28, 28, 0.3) 100%)`.
- **Specular Ambient Glows:** Active elements project a subtle gilded aura: `box-shadow: 0 12px 32px -4px rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.18), 0 4px 12px rgba(107, 5, 21, 0.4)`.

## Shapes

The geometric architecture is uncompromisingly architectural, sharp, and razor-finished (`roundedness: 0`). 

- **Sharp Edges:** All buttons, panels, modal dialogs, and image frames carry 0px border-radius, referencing tailored lapels, faceted perfume bottles (e.g., YSL Libre, Tuxedo), and beveled gold ingots.
- **Subtle Chamfers:** Exclusive celebratory cards may feature a 45-degree corner notch (4px–8px) to evoke traditional French ticket stubs and invitation stationery.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Couture Action:** Razor-cut rectangle with a burnished `#D4AF37` metallic background, `#0A0708` bold tracked typography, and a hairline highlight along the top edge. On hover, transitions into an illuminated liquid-champagne glow with an inset gold shimmer.
- **Secondary Lacquer Action:** Jet `#0A0708` interior framed by a 1px solid `#D4AF37` border, typography set in `#FBFBFB` uppercase. On hover, background shifts to `rgba(107, 5, 21, 0.45)`.
- **Ghost Action:** No border, tracked label in `#D4AF37` accompanied by an ultra-fine trailing arrow that elongates on hover.

### Chips & Badges
- Sharp rectangular capsules with 1px border `rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.4)`, background in `rgba(10, 7, 8, 0.8)`. Text rendered in `label-caps-sm` with `#FFDF73`. Used for VIP tiers, table designations, and product shade tags.

### Input Fields
- Understated, ultra-luxurious bottom-border-only or translucent-fill inputs. Baseline 1px border in `rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.3)`. Upon focus, the border intensifies to solid `#D4AF37` accompanied by a localized soft gold baseline glow. Text in `#FBFBFB`, placeholder in `rgba(251, 251, 251, 0.35)`.

### Checkboxes & Radios
- Sharp, square controls (no rounded corners). Custom 1px `#D4AF37` frame. Checked state displays an internal solid gold diamond or high-precision gilded core.

### Cards & Panels
- Multi-layered composition: Dark velvet glass backplate (`rgba(20, 9, 12, 0.75)`), 1px gradient gold perimeter border, and high-gloss specular highlight on the upper edge.

### Specialized Gala Elements
- **Stardust Shimmer Scrims:** Optional micro-particle overlays delivering subtle suspended festive gold particles across hero backdrops.
- **VIP Seal:** Interactive metallic wax-stamp toggle that reveals itinerary secrets when engaged.
